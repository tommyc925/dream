Standing room.

Dreams don’t get built by dreamers alone. This room is for everyone who wants a hand in the world they’re going to build — and there’s more than one way in, all below.

Lending a hand?

If you’d like to help the dreamers build, write I can help. Tell us how you’d want to help — a skill, an intro, a convo, an hour — or just that you’re willing. Nobody knows in advance what a dreamer will need, so willingness is a complete letter.

What you’re signing up for: we keep your letter, and when a dreamer needs something you might have, we write to you with the specific ask — or put the question to the whole room. Every ask is yours to take or leave, and any limits you name in your letter — time, topics, how often we knock — will be respected.

Know a dreamer?

If a ticket reached you but belongs to someone else, pass it on — the card says admit one dreamer, not admit the first holder. Handing it to the right person finishes the delivery; tell us it traveled and the number keeps its story.

No ticket to give? Ask for one. Tell us a little about who it’s for — the seeing is the gift, and you’ve already done that part. We’ll mail you one or two.

Cheering somebody on?

Maybe you gave a dreamer their ticket; maybe your dreamer sent you here. Either way, you’re the cheering section. Write with their ticket number — whatever you send waits at the door, and they get it the next time they knock. A told dream grows on witnesses; some of the best mail arrives before the dreamer does.

Just watching?

Good seats. Write keep me posted and that’s exactly what happens: when there’s something worth telling, one of us writes the room a letter — informal, irregular, and only when there’s something to say.
