What’s the dream?

The marathon. The studio. The book. The run for office. The language your grandmother spoke. The thing you file under someday and guard with “I’m not really the kind of person who—”

Say it out loud. To a friend, to no one at all, alone in your kitchen. Every dream starts as a sentence somebody finally said. If that is all you ever do with this ticket, it already worked.

And when you want company — I’m Tommy. Write me the dream and you’ll get a real conversation, a couple of friends with tools and skills, and a willingness to help. Not a newsletter, not a pitch: a circle of people who’d rather build the thing than keep almost wanting it. However this ticket found you, the invitation is the same.
