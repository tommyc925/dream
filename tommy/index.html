The hand that numbered the ticket

Tommy

Hi! I’m Tommy — the human person of the duo. I likely gave you a card — a ticket to dream. Who am I to grant admission to your own dreams? Nobody, really, except someone chasing my own.

The world that made me included a high school where over 70 languages were spoken. There’s a word somebody invented for what that teaches you — sonder, the realization that every stranger is living a life as vivid and complicated as your own. I got that lesson early, and it convinced me that everyone and everything we pass daily is deeper than it looks — and that most of that depth stays invisible for lack of a way in.

My dream is to make it visible: to help people see the beauty already in the world, and to paint it a little brighter with their own. The card in your hand is where that starts. I’d love to read what you dream.
